📚 Financial Tips Unlocked

💡

Pay Yourself First: Automatically save a portion of income before spending. Even ₹500/month adds up to ₹6,000/year!

📊

50/30/20 Rule: Allocate 50% to needs, 30% to wants, and 20% to savings.

🚫

Avoid Lifestyle Inflation: When your income grows, resist the urge to upgrade everything immediately.

⚡

Rule of 72: Divide 72 by your annual interest rate to find how many years your money takes to double. At 8%, that's just 9 years!

💸

Beat Inflation: Inflation (~6%) doubles the price of goods every 12 years. If your savings earn less than inflation, you're losing real purchasing power.
